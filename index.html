Our Mission

Nepal is rich in Diplopod fauna. After compiling the checklist of millipedes, we found that the whole western and southern parts are unexplored. All the species identified on the basis of morphology. Integrative taxonomy may also discover new species from explored area as well as unexplored parts. Our mission is to advance the understanding of millipede biodiversity in Nepal through comprehensive field collection from diverse geographical regions, followed by accurate species-level identification using integrated morphological and molecular analysis, detailed documentation, and long-term specimen preservation.

Nepal's Elevation Gradient: From Terai to Everest

Nepal's diverse topography creates unique microhabitats for millipede species across different elevations.
