Nepalese Millipede Species Checklist

Complete list of millipede species documented from Nepal (1935-2025)

Methodology

This checklist is based on primary as well as secondary information. Millipede species of Nepal published in various literature were listed including their distribution locations. Articles were searched from 'Google Scholar', taxonomic journal websites such as Zootaxa and Zookeys as well as Zoological records using keywords 'millipedes', 'nepal', 'diplopoda'. Old literature was downloaded from Biodiversity Heritage Library and Archive.org.

For species records in Nepal, specimens were observed during field visits in Eastern and central parts of Nepal. All higher rankings were placed in alphabetical orders. After tabulating all the species in the table, confirmed in 'Milli-Base' for their classification (Sierwald & Spelda 2025).
