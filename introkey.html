What are Millipedes?

Millipedes are arthropods belonging to the class Diplopoda. They are characterized by having two pairs of jointed legs on most body segments. With over 12,000 species worldwide, they are one of the most diverse groups of organisms on Earth. Millipedes play a vital role in ecosystems as decomposers, breaking down decaying plant material and recycling nutrients back into the soil.
